## Phase Goal

**As a** solo owner, **I want to** run an Arabic RTL app on real phones with a sync-ready database and proven native integrations, **so that** feature work never rests on guesses.

<objective>
Establish the app's NativeWind 4 theme tokens with a warm, paper-like palette (D-07), configure the single MMKV instance (`services/storage.ts`), and wire an accessible light/dark/system switch on the home screen (FND-05).

Purpose: isolates styling/MMKV configuration from font downloads and Reusables CLI installs (plan 01-09), keeping every plan small and easy to bisect on device.
Output: `tailwind.config.js`, `global.css`, `nativewind-env.d.ts`, MMKV preference service, `constants/theme.ts`, `use-theme-preference.ts`, and the home theme switch.
</objective>
