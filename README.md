# روعي (Ro'i)

An Arabic Christian companion app for iOS and Android.

Ro'i keeps your daily walk with God in one place so verses, prayers, and gratitude don't get buried in scattered notes. The name has two meanings in Arabic: *my shepherd* and *cared for*.

> «كلمتك، وهويتك، وصلاتك… في مكان واحد، وحد بيرعاك وإنت جواه.»

It includes four tools:

- **ليا أنا**: Weekly and monthly verses picked for you, names of God, and blessings addressed to you by name.
- **حفظ الآيات**: Memorize verses at your own pace with spaced review, voice recording, and recall exercises.
- **تشفع**: Keep a prayer list by person, notice who has waited longest, and archive answered prayers.
- **دفتر الشكر**: Write one line of gratitude each night.

After the first download, the app works offline. Everything personal stays on your device unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive or suggest a verse for others. Home-screen widgets and daily reminders keep what is due today easy to find.
